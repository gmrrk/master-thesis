\begin{abstract}

Image compression algorithms are ubiquitous in modern computer systems and
lossy codecs save exabytes of memory across the internet by eliminating redundant visual data.
Traditional transforms such as DCT and DWT divide images into fixed or variable-size blocks,
applying quantization and entropy encoding to each.
However, harsher compression often results in visible block artifacts that penalize the overall quality of compressed image.
This thesis proposes a novel fractal-based compression algorithm FRIF that replaces rectangular blocks with complex-based fractals
arranged in a hexagonal tiling pattern. The hexagonal structure provides six immediate neighbors, enabling enhanced multidimensional
statistical modeling and prediction, which aims to reduce block artifacts and improve compression quality.
